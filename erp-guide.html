End to End

The Production Pipeline

Cloth flows from supplier to buyer through linked records. Each stage creates a document that connects to the next.

Supplier→ Purchase Order→ Raw Cloth Batch→ Cutting→ Stitching→ Finished Product→ Sales Order→ Payment / Credit
Flow 1 — Cloth Procurement
1
Add Supplier
Create with contact, GSTIN, supply type, credit days. One-time setup.
2
Create Purchase Order
Select supplier, add cloth items (category + color + meters) or readymade items. Auto-number: PO-YYYYMM-NNNN.
3
Confirm PO
Status: Draft → Confirmed. Locks the order for receiving.
4
Receive PO + Parcel Inspection
Record actual meters received per line. Log parcel condition, quantity check result, discrepancy notes. Received-by employee stamped.
5
Raw Cloth Batch Auto-Created
A batch record links to the PO with bin location, cost per meter, and available meters ready for cutting.
Flow 2 — Production (Cut → Stitch → Finish)
1
Create Cutting Assignment
Pick a cloth batch, assign cutting master, set item type, size, meters, target pieces. Auto-number: CA-YYYYMM-NNNN.
2
Update Cutting Progress
Record pieces completed, cloth used, cloth wasted. Cost per piece and wastage % auto-calculated.
3
Create Stitching Job
Link to the cutting assignment, assign tailor, set pieces to stitch. Auto-number: SJ-YYYYMM-NNNN.
4
Update Stitching Progress
Record pieces completed and pieces rejected. Mark completed when done.
5
Create Finished Product
Add to finished goods with SKU, barcode (auto-generated), cost price, sale price, size, warehouse.
Flow 3 — Quotation → Sales → Credit
1
Create Quotation
Select buyer, add finished product items with prices. Apply discount and GST. Print or share. Status: Draft → Sent → Accepted / Rejected / Expired.
2
Convert to Sales Order
One click on accepted quotation. Choose CREDIT (creates credit transaction) or PAID.
3
Record Payments
For credit orders: record partial payments (cash / cheque / NEFT / UPI) until balance is zero. Status: OUTSTANDING → PARTIAL → SETTLED.
Flow 4 — Walk-in Purchase Bill (No PO needed)
1
Create Purchase Bill
For direct cash/credit purchases. Auto-number: PB-YYYYMM-NNNN.
2
Add Line Items
Cloth or readymade items with quantity, unit price, bin location.
3
Record Supplier Payments
Pay in instalments. Bill status updates automatically: UNPAID → PARTIAL → PAID.

Procurement

Suppliers

Businesses that supply cloth and readymade stock to the warehouse.

Supplier ManagementCRUD

Full create / edit / deactivate. Each record stores contact person, GSTIN, WhatsApp, supply type (Cloth / Readymade / Both), and credit days.

  • Add and edit suppliers with full contact and GSTIN details
  • Filter list by supply type
  • Deactivate — stays in history, removed from new order dropdowns
  • WhatsApp field: "Same as phone" toggle inline with label

Procurement

Buyers

Customers who purchase finished garments. Credit limits and buyer type drive how sales and credit are handled.

Buyer ManagementCRUD

Manage buyers with type (Wholesale / Retail / Export), credit limit, GSTIN, and WhatsApp. Credit limit enforced on credit sales.

  • Add and edit buyers with GSTIN, address, credit limit
  • Filter by buyer type
  • Outstanding credit balance visible from Dashboard
  • WhatsApp field: "Same as phone" toggle inline with label
  • Sidebar shows red badge count when any credit is overdue
Procurement

Purchase Orders

Formal orders placed before cloth arrives. Tracks ordered vs received quantities through a full receiving workflow.

PO LifecycleFull Workflow

Draft → Confirmed → Partially Received → Received. Each transition is timestamped with the acting employee.

  • Auto-number: PO-YYYYMM-NNNN
  • Add cloth items (category + color + meters) or readymade items per line
  • Confirm to lock for receiving; receive to record actual quantities
  • Parcel inspection: condition, quantity check, discrepancy notes, inspected-by
  • Created-by and received-by employee names displayed on detail view
  • Sidebar badge: count of active (unreceived) POs
Procurement

Purchase Bills

Walk-in or direct purchases with no formal PO. Full GST Input Tax Credit tracking and payment history against each bill.

Bills & Supplier PaymentsFinance
  • Auto-number: PB-YYYYMM-NNNN
  • Add cloth or readymade line items with bin location
  • Payment status: UNPAID → PARTIAL → PAID (auto-updated)
  • Record Payment modal: amount, date, mode, reference number
  • Full payment history shown in bill detail panel
  • Supplier payable totals flow to Dashboard KPI tiles
  • Print button — formatted purchase bill with all GST breakdowns
GST Input Tax CreditNew

Controlled by the Apply GST on Purchase Bills toggle in Settings. Zero overhead when off — enabling it activates per-item GST rates and automatic ITC split.

  • Per-item GST rate selector: 0 / 5 / 12 / 18 / 28 %
  • Live "line total + X% GST = ₹Y" preview as you type
  • Intra-state supplier → CGST + SGST (50/50 split)
  • Inter-state supplier → IGST (full amount)
  • State determined by supplier.state vs Settings company state
  • Bill totals: Taxable Amount · Tax Amount · Grand Total
  • CGST / SGST / IGST chips shown in bill detail view
  • Company GSTIN printed on purchase bill printout

Operations

Custom Roles

SUPER_ADMIN creates named roles with a per-tab permission grid. Assign a custom role to any employee to override their default tab visibility.

Role BuilderAdmin

Each custom role is a named set of checkboxes — one per application tab. The employee sees only the tabs their custom role permits.

  • Create / edit / delete custom roles (SUPER_ADMIN only)
  • 26-tab permission grid — toggle each tab on or off per role
  • Assign a custom role to one or more employees via the Employees tab
  • Custom role badge displayed beside the employee's name in the list
  • Backend mutation permissions are unchanged — only tab visibility is controlled
  • An employee without a custom role falls back to their system role's default visibility
How it interacts with System RolesNote

Two independent layers. The system role (ADMIN, MANAGER, etc.) governs what the backend allows — which mutations succeed and which warehouses are visible. The custom role governs what the frontend shows — which tabs appear in the sidebar. You can give a STORE_KEEPER a custom role that hides Purchase Orders while still letting them receive stock, or give a MANAGER a restricted view showing only Cutting and Stitching.

Settings

System-wide configuration. Visible to SUPER_ADMIN only. All changes take effect immediately after saving.

App & CompanyConfig
  • App name and subtitle — shown in browser tab and sidebar header
  • Company name and state — determines CGST/SGST vs IGST split on sales and purchases
  • Company GSTIN — printed on purchase bills when GST is enabled
  • Currency symbol, default GST %, OTP expiry, allow OTP login
  • Apply GST on Purchase Bills toggle — enables per-item GST rates and CGST/SGST/IGST Input Tax Credit tracking on all purchase bills
Brand ColorsConfig
  • Primary color: sidebar, buttons, headings (live color picker)
  • Accent color: badges and highlights (live color picker)
  • Live preview strip shows the chosen palette before saving
  • Applied instantly across the entire UI on save
Print & Document LayoutNew
  • Company address — appears in header of all printed documents
  • Bank details — printed in footer of invoices and quotations
  • Terms & conditions — numbered list on printed documents
  • Signature line label (e.g. "Authorised Signatory")
  • Show / hide company logo on printed documents
IntegrationsConfig
  • Email: SMTP host, port, user, password, from address, enable toggle
  • SMS: Twilio SID, auth token, from number, enable toggle
  • WhatsApp: Meta Business API token, phone number ID, enable toggle
  • Push: Firebase service account JSON, enable toggle

Print & Document Layout

Four document types have a dedicated print layout. Click the 🖨 Print button on any record — a formatted page opens in a new tab and the browser print dialog fires automatically. All shared content (logo, address, bank details, T&Cs) is configured once in Settings → Print & Document Layout.

QuotationPrint
  • Company header with logo (optional) and address
  • Buyer info (name, phone, GSTIN, address) + warehouse info
  • Itemised table: product, size, quantity, unit price, total
  • Subtotal · discount · GST · grand total
  • Terms & conditions · bank details · signature line
Purchase OrderPrint
  • PO number, date, expected delivery in header
  • Supplier name and contact details
  • Line items: category, colour, item type, quantity, unit price, total
  • Grand total and payment terms
  • Signature line from Settings
Sales OrderPrint
  • SO number, date, dispatch date in header
  • Buyer details: name, phone, GSTIN, state, address
  • Items: product, size, quantity, unit price, GST, total
  • Subtotal · CGST/SGST (or IGST) · discount · grand total
  • Bank details · terms & conditions · signature line
Purchase BillPrint

Only visible when GST on Purchase Bills is enabled in Settings; the GST columns are omitted otherwise.

  • Bill number, date, company GSTIN in header
  • Supplier name and details
  • Items: description, quantity, rate, GST %, taxable, tax, line total
  • Taxable amount · CGST/SGST or IGST · grand total
  • Payment history and outstanding balance
